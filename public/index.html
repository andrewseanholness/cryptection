    [ THE ENGINE ]

    How Cryptection Works

    We don't just guess risk scores. Our serverless architecture aggregates real blockchain data and applies proprietary behavioral rules to spot scams before they rug.

    1

    Data Ingestion (APIs)

    When an address is submitted, our Vercel backend pings public BSC/ETH RPC Nodes and Explorers to fetch raw transaction history and contract bytecode.

    2

    Security Enrichment

    We run the bytecode against specialized endpoints (like GoPlus) to check for known malicious signatures, honeypots, or hidden tax mechanisms.

    3

    Heuristic Engine

    Our behavioral rules take over. Did the dev get funded by Tornado Cash? Have they launched 5 failed tokens previously? The engine scores these actions.

    4

    Threat Graphing

    If Wallet A is flagged as a scammer, and sends funds to Wallet B, Wallet B's Network Risk instantly skyrockets across our ecosystem database.
